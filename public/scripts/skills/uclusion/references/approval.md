# Question owner: job approval

Approve only an Approvable job with settled assistance and no existing AI job approval. Assess its business/value premise against evidence and related work. Ask missing questions and make relevant suggestions first; weak, untested or contradicted premises merit lower certainty.

Required AI approval proceeds once these conditions hold. For optional approval, use the question role's existing next-action question. Use `approve_job_or_option` with integer certainty 1–5 and the reason for the value assessment.

Approval records business value. Execution and manual stage changes retain their own permission requirements.
