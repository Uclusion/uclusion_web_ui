## Select current contract and evidence

Determine exact target/owner/current contract yourself. Context uses add_info on its current R-code; absent capsule uses ordinary target notes. Independent tasks never fall back to job contracts; grouped work uses top-level parent; verification-produced work retains originating contract and named delta.

Sent bodies change only for later human input establishing a new contract. A newer clear non-AI, non-advisory human answer conflicting with the current capsule is capsule-changing input, including a reply. Stop affected execution, clarify any missing decision and publish the settled change before resuming. Never treat it as no-change settlement with included_in_capsule:false. Newly discovered older input requires a question. Record the standalone actor-visible contract before further affected edits or lane end, even without edits; settled changes cannot remain beside contradictory old bodies. Missing capsule needs relevant ordinary-note evidence and isolated investigation. Settle reviewer-divergent choices with informed human approval, never silent defaults.

Qualify answers/replies/votes under job-assistance, including required replies/votes and advisory markers. Retain canonical URLs, question/option text, exact selected qualified code/name, settled outcome, handled rendered version/pending state and every required target. No-change resolutions belong to the question role, not publication. Preserve all standing-note constraints and action-specific human authorization. Search history in fresh export-search before design work; do not load its algorithm yourself.

## Publish and recover

Compose, cold-review and publish within the actual settled human authority using the sibling design role's publication rules. Include named capsule-changing answered-question resolutions, including delegated human-resolved handling, only after every required target is confirmed. Earlier targets publish without acknowledgement; final target can acknowledge once all others confirm, including unchanged-body publication. Incorporation requires delegated:false and included_in_capsule:true. Unfinished resolutions resume only after all intended publications confirm.

Inspect separate publication/question/obsolete-review outcomes. Reconcile uncertain writes with scoped reads and actual evidence; retry only unfinished operations, never a confirmed publication after later failure or an unseen replacement version. A stale acknowledgement needs evidence reconciliation, never a fresh version pasted into retry. Missing/stale/unsettled evidence requires the permitted question or next role, not broadened scope.

After an AI replacement resolve each obsolete AI review named by its result; failed inventory fetch selects Reports. For human capsule edits read current R-thread and Reports, resolve your open review naming it first and reconcile affected work before returning ready current identities/requirements/permissions. Later cold review never polishes a sent body. Use stage_only after the last operation when needed without overwriting newer events; publication grants no implementation or transition.

Return only canonical target/link/R-code/actual-version receipts, per-question version/delegated/included outcomes, obsolete-review outcomes, confirmed/unfinished recovery, exact missing human decisions/permissions and next mechanical handoff. Pending/failed/unconfirmed operations are not ready. Keep full bodies and research in the capsule helper.
