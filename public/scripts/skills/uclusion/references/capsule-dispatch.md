# Coordinator capsule design and publication

## Capsule authority

Use job-context.md's exact target/current-contract prerequisite. New context uses add_info on its current R-code; absent capsule uses an ordinary target note. Independent tasks never fall back to job capsules; grouped tasks use top-level parent.

Sent bodies change only for later human input establishing a new contract. Newly discovered older input requires a question. Record the new standalone actor-visible contract before further affected edits or lane end, even without edits; settled changes cannot remain beside contradictory old bodies.

## Bounded design dispatch and publication

For absent capsules, isolate investigation, settle reviewer-divergent choices and supply relevant ordinary-note evidence. Delegate planning/composition/revision/cold review/publication fresh with `fork_turns: "none"`, selected sibling ../uclusion-design/SKILL.md resolved from the selected uclusion package root, target, bounded settled evidence/constraints/new human requirements and either confirmed absence or full current body/R-code/actual version. Never send coordinator bodies. Impose no numeric cap; do not read helper-only instructions.

For referenced questions supply canonical URL, relevant question/option text, exact selected qualified code/name, settled outcome, handled rendered version/pending state and every required target. Qualify answers/replies/votes yourself under job-assistance.md. Include every listed view-note R-code/current version and exact thread-read grant under helper-dispatch.md. Grant mutations only for exact creation/permitted replacement, named capsule-changing answered-question resolutions including human-resolved pending handling, and replacement obsolete-AI-review cleanup. No-change resolutions remain yours. Earlier targets publish without acknowledgement. Final-target publication may acknowledge once all OTHER required targets confirm, including unchanged-body publication; grant unfinished resolutions separately only after ALL publications confirm.

Accept only canonical target/link/R-code/actual-version publication receipt, per-question version/delegated/included_in_capsule outcomes, obsolete-review outcomes and unfinished recovery, typed unresolved questions, or concrete coordinator-action constraints with exact view-note R-code/version. Incorporation requires delegated:false and included_in_capsule:true, not merely resolved. Pending, failed or unconfirmed publisher operations are not ready. Return only unfinished work or those note constraints; missing/stale scope/evidence returns questions, not broadened grants. Publication grants neither implementation, stage nor ownership. Missing instructions require broken-install report, environment update permission and restart/reconnect, never improvised publication.

After publication use current-contract reference prerequisite and, when needed, stage_only after the publisher's last operation without overwriting newer stage. Later cold review cannot polish a sent body. Conflicts/uncertain outcomes reconcile scoped state and renew exact settled authority; inspect separate outcomes/retry only unfinished operations under durable-records.md. Human capsule edits refresh current R-thread/Reports, resolve your open review naming it first, then reconcile affected work. Publisher cleanup never replaces that human-edit route.
