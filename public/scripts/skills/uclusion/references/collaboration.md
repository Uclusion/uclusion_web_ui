# Collaboration

Apply this procedure within your selected role and assignment, using that role's discussion, capsule and coordinator handoff procedures.

Before designing or editing this skill's instructions, read `README.md` beside the source `SKILL.md` in the checkout being changed.

Obtain the human's understanding and approval of material design choices, including internal implementation choices. Your confidence, a familiar convention or one workable approach does not establish the human's decision.

Read the current source and earlier decisions before choosing an approach or accepting a supplied design. Identify material alternatives a reasonable human could choose differently. Consider observable behavior and relevant internal choices, such as where state lives, its keying and lifecycle, formats and interfaces, and failure or concurrency behavior. Use these examples to recognize choices, without a fixed inventory or question quota.

Work through the behavior or content central to the requested result far enough to show what happens or what the user sees, using concrete examples or outputs where relevant. Goals, topics and acceptance checks cannot substitute for the approach itself or leave the implementer to invent it. Bring material choices exposed by this work into the question process before settling the approach.

Reuse settled human requirements, qualifying answers, accepted suggestions and explicit delegation of a decision. Source constraints and prior authoritative records can establish facts; they do not supply human approval for a choice that remains open. Do not ask again about an unchanged answered decision or seek an operational approval already held. Keep routine execution within the agreed design.

Raise material missing facts, unsettled choices or permissions through your role's discussion procedures before settling the approach or implementing affected behavior. Explain the real-world choice and consequences in plain English, offering concrete options and an explained recommendation for genuine discrete choices. State the evidence and tradeoffs behind the recommendation, distinguishing facts from assumptions or unmeasured preferences. Do not treat a preferred default as an agreed decision or defer a choice to implementation or review. An unanswered choice blocks work that depends on it while independent authorized work continues. Preserve independent human permissions and approved type, quantity and scope for anything you or your subagents run.
