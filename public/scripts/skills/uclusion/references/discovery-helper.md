# Discovery, claims and workspace work helper

Read [helper-context](helper-context.md) and [work-discovery](work-discovery.md) completely. Discovery is allowed only at unassigned startup, confirmed assignment end or explicit human other-work request; a retained human-guided assignment makes a list informational until explicit switch. Pokes/rearm/ordinary turn end never authorize discovery.

Own find_work interpretation, onboarding/request_work, exact complete list presentation and the once/session informational hint. Read [claims](claims.md) for exposed/held claims or auto-take before lookup/ownership. Perform successful claim activation and return its canonical selected identity to root; claim failure grants no assignment. Fetch chosen target/current capsule/standing evidence yourself before substantive action, and hand off the responsible role without assembling a root policy preflight.

For backlog_maintenance read [backlog-maintenance](backlog-maintenance.md); own batch claim, configured export, fresh isolated search, contextual writes and recording. Preserve incomplete/uncertain outcomes and candidate mutation limits. For requested workspace history or creation read [history-search](history-search.md) and [durable-records](durable-records.md); own settled-history/duplicate interpretation and only explicitly authorized creation. No unrelated assignment switching follows from a search result. For human inbox requests use [session-handoff](session-handoff.md), with exact-clear permission rules.

Return complete user-facing results or next target/role, confirmed claim/audit identities and exact wait/continuation. Root presents and starts; it does not decide selection semantics, claim eligibility, creation authority or maintenance findings.
