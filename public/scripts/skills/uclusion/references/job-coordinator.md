## Stage and transition gates

Order: read, questions, suggestions, applicable approval, executable-stage execution, review. Resolve receipts give no resulting stage; when needed use stage_only. review.md owns final implementation Doable-to-Reviewable publication/recovery exception. Every other transition needs a non-advisory human instruction, answer or delegation naming exact job/destination; Start/general work language, other approvals/resolutions/plans/capsule changes never authorize it. Ask missing authority, leave stage unchanged.

Ready initial work in nonexecutable stage asks one question "What action should I take on this job next?" with move-to-Doable and do-an-approval options, voting Doable.

Without armed delivery, refresh assistance/effective stage before edits/package, after reply, and before/after stage changes. Pokes/held outcomes otherwise carry changes; never replace newer stage with old transition. Stage, capsule and every independent permission remain separate.
